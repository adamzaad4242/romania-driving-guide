Driving in Romania in 2027: the vignette went fully electronic on 1 October 2026

Planning a Romania road trip in 2027? One thing changed this autumn that is worth knowing before you set off. Since 1 October 2026 the Romanian rovinieta is fully electronic. There is no sticker for the windscreen anymore. It is tied to your registration plate and checked by cameras, so buy it on etoll.ro before you drive.

What changed on 1 October 2026

Law 226/2023 replaced the old paper vignette system. The rovinieta is now an electronic record linked to your plate number. Cameras on national roads and motorways read plates and check validity automatically. You do not need to show anything at the border or stick anything on the windscreen.

This applies to cars driving on national roads and motorways across Romania. If you are hiring a car in Romania, the hire company normally includes a valid rovinieta, but it is worth asking them to confirm before you drive off.

How long a rovinieta lasts

For a car, you can buy a rovinieta for 1 day, 10 days, 30 days, 60 days or 12 months. Buy it before you start driving, not after you have already entered a tolled road. The purchase is made on the official portal etoll.ro, and you can also buy at border crossings and most petrol stations.

One detail that catches people out: the vignette is linked to the plate number you type in. Check the number twice. A single wrong letter means the system sees your car as driving without a vignette, even though you paid.

The fine if you drive without one

The fine for driving without a valid rovinieta is no longer a fixed sum. Under art. 18 para. (2) of Law 226/2023 it is between 2x and 4x the 12 month tariff for the vehicle category. For a car that is a serious amount, and it is easy to avoid by buying the vignette first and checking that it is valid.

The Fetesti bridge is free now

The Fetesti-Cernavoda bridge crossing is free both ways now. There is no separate bridge toll to pay on top of the vignette. If you are driving from Bucharest toward the Black Sea coast, that is one less thing to worry about.

Motorcycles are exempt

Motorcycles do not need a rovinieta in Romania. This is one of the few exemptions in the system, and it is worth knowing if you are planning a bike trip through the Carpathians.

Check before you drive

Before a trip, it takes less than a minute to check whether a car has a valid vignette. You can check a car's current validity free on Verificare Rovinieta.
